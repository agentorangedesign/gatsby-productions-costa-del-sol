Who We Are

Gatsby Productions brings 20 years of entertainment excellence to the Costa del Sol. We have evolved from South Africa's premier events company to a trusted partner for discerning clients throughout Marbella, Estepona, and the wider Costa del Sol region.

We specialise in creating unforgettable experiences through carefully curated entertainment, professional event management, and an unmatched roster of talented performers.
